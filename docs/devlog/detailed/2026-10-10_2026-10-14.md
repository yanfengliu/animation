# Devlog: 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-14

One work unit, `docs/work/0_hello-world-cloud/`, ran over five days. A coordinator session directed fifteen workers (W1 to W15) and seven pinned Claude review rounds (0–6). Two owner directives changed the design midway: no angle-dependent lighting, then slanted letters. The repo joined the fleet on the first night. API limits stopped workers four times; each time their files survived and a fresh worker landed them.

The failures had one shape: a check passing for a reason other than the one it existed for. Twice it was a mutation tool counting a harness error as a kill. Once it was a mask cut from the render it was judging. Once it was a reveal effect making the text readable by lighting rather than by geometry. Three times it was a check of one property (light, then warmth) that a reveal could slip past by varying another.

## An exact pixel identity closes what one-property checks kept missing

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-14 (`cea1d4e`, `2056eac`, `b0a1c40`)

**Action:**
- Rounds 3, 4 and 5 each found one more property that a future reveal could vary past the alike gate: the shipped draw, then colour, then camera mode and dwell.
- After round 4 the coordinator stopped adding one property per round. W12 and W13 tied the shipped render to the test-only layers pixel by pixel, per channel: shipped − sky = (text − sky) + (ambient − sky), capped at 255. They held camera modes and an 8 s dwell to pixel identity with E set by URL.
- W14 and W15 replaced the warmth comparison with red, green and blue checked separately.

**Result:**
- The identity measures exactly 0 on the real page for seeds 1–3.
- A gold tint in the shipped draw only gives 2.5–3.6%, against a 0.1% bound.
- A green tint that keeps light and warmth gives 0.73/1.10/0.74 per channel, against a 0.97–1.03 band.
- The final proof had a green control run, then killed all 74 mutants.

**Reasoning:** ONE/ONE blending, no depth buffer and per-sprite depth of field make the shipped draw exactly the sum of its two layers. So any shipped-only effect shows as a nonzero residual, whatever property it varies.

**Validation:**
- The identity rests on SwiftShader not dithering; WebGL dithers by default.
- Reveals between measured angles, on a running clock or on a bare URL remain outside the gates. Each is named in AGENTS.md's bound.
- The fleet's repair cap (five rounds) was reached at round 5. Round 6 reported only.

## Lighting that helps the reader is cheating; the words come from positions alone

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-11 13:57 to 23:37

**Action:** the owner rejected the reveal as cheating. Near the secret eye E, text particles had brightened, turned gold, grown and come into focus, while ambient particles dimmed. W8 removed every angle-dependent difference between text and ambient particles, and retuned β from 0.2 to 0.85 and the aperture to 0.14. W8 also added a test-only `beta` parameter whose `beta=0` twin isolates relocation. W9 replaced the reveal gate with "every particle renders alike at every angle", which compares each particle drawn as text with the same particle drawn as ambient, at E, 3° off, 25° off and the back.

**Result:** the stroke/gap ratio at E fell from 2.35–2.50 (lit) to 1.61 (positions alone), and the words still read at a glance. Four reveal mutants (brighter, gold, bigger, sharper near E) are each killed by the alike gate. This is commit `6642bc7`.

**Reasoning:** the reveal had been added to make up for β 0.2, which was itself chosen to hide row streaks. Removing it meant buying legibility with more relocated particles and accepting more structure off-angle.

**Validation:** the full proof had a green control run and killed all 55 mutants. The defect is recorded in `docs/learning/defect-register.md`.

## Turning the camera about one axis never blurs the hidden text along that axis

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-11 00:19 to 14:35

**Action:** W2, then W8, measured the high-passed row and column residual of render(β) − render(0) against the cloud's own noise floor.

**Result:** at β 0.85 the az ±25 rows sit near the floor (1.25 and 1.02 against 1.17). The el ±25 columns did not: 1.74 and 2.20 against 0.88, with visible vertical stem streaks. The full table across β, stroke width and particle count is in the plan.

**Reasoning:** an azimuth turn at E's 14° elevation adds roll of about Δaz·sin 14°, which washes the rows out. An elevation turn adds no roll, so every text particle keeps its screen column. Spreading particles in depth cannot change that.

**Validation:** W9's relocation-residual gate covers az ±25, az +60 and the back; `no-density-compensation` turns it red. Elevation ±15–25° is an accepted, documented limit, not gated as hidden.

## Slanted letters spread the stems that an elevation turn keeps

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-12 00:58

**Action:** at the owner's request, W10 sheared the glyphs (14°) by leaning each mask sample back upright about its line's baseline. The layout gate now unslants the mask before measuring, and a new `upright-letters` mutant is killed by it.

**Result:**
- The el ±25 column residual went from 1.79× and 2.26× the floor to 1.26× and 1.72× (seed 1), a 34% cut on average over seeds 1–3. The E ratio held at 1.607, against 1.611 before.
- By eye, el ±25 now looks like a plain cloud.
- The other slants failed: 18° broke the hidden-from-25° margin (0.067), and 22° broke the streak bound (1.48×).
- This is commit `9c0425f`.

**Reasoning:** a slanted stem covers many screen columns, so an elevation turn smears it sideways instead of keeping it as one bright column.

**Validation:** in W10's full proof, 55 mutants were killed and 1 survived: `full-relocation` (β 1.0). With slanted letters, β 1.0 measures no streakier than β 0.85 at the gated views, so the coordinator retired that mutant and documented that nothing pins β below 1.

## A contrast mask rebuilt from the render under test confirms itself

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-11 01:16 to 08:28

**Action:** W3 added a cross-seed stroke IoU to the legibility gate. W9 later added a lift over the same view's `beta=0` twin.

**Result:**
- With relocation removed, the stroke/gap ratio still read 2.35, against 2.40 for the real page.
- Cross-seed IoU separates them: the real page scores at least 0.50, the mutant at most 0.29.
- At β 0.85 the honest page's ratio without relocation is still 1.52, so the gate also requires relocation to lift the ratio 1.35×.

**Reasoning:** scattered particles light whatever mask is cut from them. Letters drawn by two different seeds overlap only when both follow the message.

**Code reviewer comments:** round 1 found that nothing checked which way the text reads, so mirrored and upside-down text passed. W6 then added layout checks.
