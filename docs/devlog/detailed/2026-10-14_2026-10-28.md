## One cloud hides six messages, each read only from its own eye

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-14 19:11 to 2026-10-21 23:41 (`733d3d9`, `8dc6893`)

**Action:**
- W16 grew the hello world cloud into one that hides six messages: "hello world", a smiling face, "What", "a", "beautiful" and "world".
- Each particle gets at most one message, by one draw over its normalized chances, and lands on that message's strokes along its own sight line from that message's eye.
- A tour at load, Play, "Find the angle" and a `#capture` mode play the sequence; capture loops it with no controls and no cursor until Escape.

**Result:**
- 48,000 particles, 30,254 of them text.
- Stroke/gap ratios at the eyes of 1.70–2.01 at 1280×800 and 1.72–1.90 at 390×844 (seed 1, in the final `npm test`), from particle positions alone; each message gets 51–64% of the particles it would claim alone.
- The coordinator's native-resolution pass over 47 views, bound to their sha256 digests, found every message legible at its eye and none legible elsewhere.

**Reasoning:**
- Every eye sits at +14° elevation or higher. An eye within about 20° of another's antipode sees that message mirrored and radially smeared: the face's smile showed under "beautiful" when the face sat at −30°.
- The face has weight 0.6, and its eyes grew from 0.55 to 0.9 face units, so they read; small eye loops cost "beautiful" 0.085, because they survive the smear near the face's antipode.
- β stays 0.85 by decision (the lesson in `docs/learning/lessons.md`).

**Validation:**
- Rendering is deterministic: W18's own shots of the same views have the same sha256 as the coordinator's, and every later repair left the 27 acceptance digests unchanged.
- Near its antipode each message still forms a bright mirrored ghost, strongest for "world" (a bowtie at (158°, −18°)) and hello world (a four-wing star at (218°, −14°)). None is legible, none lies on the capture path, and `MAX_GHOST_LIFT` now holds them near today's level.

**Notes:** The live artifact holds this page as version 4.
